## The problem

Help customers turn reputation data into actionable intelligence through a production AI agent.

## My role

As Principal Data Scientist at Reputation.com from April 2025 to November 2026, I architected and launched RepIQ. My work combined technical leadership, AI/ML experimentation, and collaboration across data science and software engineering.

## The approach

RepIQ used agentic retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and LLM evaluation frameworks. I mentored six data scientists and collaborated with seven software engineers as part of the broader role.

## Results

By November 2026, the product served customers across 311 companies and reached more than 2,600 frequent users, while maintaining low-latency responses and a negative-feedback rate below 0.05%.
