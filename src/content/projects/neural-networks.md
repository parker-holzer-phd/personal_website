## The exercise

Build and train a neural network while keeping the example small enough to inspect.

## Inside the notebook

The notebook generates a synthetic two-dimensional classification dataset, defines a multilayer network with PyTorch, and trains it using cross-entropy loss and the Adam optimizer. It then inspects predictions and visualizes the predicted classes.

## The visualization

The figure is the notebook’s saved prediction output. This is an educational example on generated data, rather than a benchmark on a production task.
